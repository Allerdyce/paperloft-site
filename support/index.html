Frequently asked questions

When will Paperloft Receipts be available?

Soon, on the Mac App Store.

Which Macs does it need?

A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 27 or later.

Does it upload my documents?

No. Paperloft Receipts reads your documents on your Mac. We never receive them.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no account to create.

Where are my files kept?

In a folder you choose, as ordinary files.

Is it tax advice?

No. Paperloft organizes records. It isn’t tax advice.
