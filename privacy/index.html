Paperloft Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Paperloft apps are made by EvidencePair LLC, California. In this policy, “we” means EvidencePair LLC.

Your documents stay on your Mac

Paperloft apps process your documents on your Mac. We don’t receive, store or see your documents or the information in them.

No analytics and no account

We don’t collect analytics, usage data or advertising identifiers. There’s no account to create.

Where your files are kept

Your files live in a folder you choose. If you put that folder in a cloud service such as iCloud Drive or Dropbox, that service’s terms and privacy policy apply to it.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We don’t receive your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Emailing support

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we don’t share them.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We set no cookies and use no analytics. GitHub may process technical data, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

Paperloft apps aren’t directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect children’s information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
