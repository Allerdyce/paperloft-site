A few things
worth knowing.

Can I download Paperloft Receipts yet?

Not yet. Paperloft Receipts is coming soon to the Mac App Store.

Do my documents leave my Mac?

Your documents are read and organized on your Mac using Apple’s on-device technology. We never receive them.

What can I send to my accountant?

Export an accountant pack for a tax year or quarter: a summary PDF, a spreadsheet file, and the documents sorted by category. Paperloft organizes records; it isn’t tax advice.

Will there be more Paperloft apps?

Yes. More Paperloft apps for your paperwork are on the way.
