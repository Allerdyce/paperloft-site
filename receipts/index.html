Paperloft Receipts · Coming soon to the Mac

Drop anything in. It’s named, filed and ready for your accountant.

Paperloft Receipts turns a pile of receipts and bills into tidy, searchable records, sorted by year and category, on your Mac.

How it works

  1. Drop in receipts and bills

    PDFs, photos, scans, email attachments.

  2. Check the suggestions

    Paperloft reads each one on your Mac and suggests a name, date, vendor, total and category. You confirm with one keystroke.

  3. Hand it over at tax time

    Export an accountant pack: a summary PDF, a spreadsheet file, and the documents sorted by category.

What it does

  • Files itself

    Renames and sorts into year and category folders.

  • Review inbox

    Fast, keyboard-first checking.

  • Accountant pack

    For any tax year or quarter.

  • Search everything

    Including the text inside scans.

  • Undo any batch

    Change your mind about a whole batch in one step.

  • Ordinary files

    Your files stay ordinary files in a folder you choose.

Private by design

  • Documents are read on your Mac using Apple’s on-device technology.
  • No account is needed.
  • No analytics.
